BOOKING APP

A SIMPLE BOOKING SYSTEM FOR APPOINTMENT-BASED BUSINESSES

Many appointment-based businesses still manage bookings through messages, notes, and manual reminders. FlowBook helps organize bookings, services, availability, and client requests in one simple place.

Common problems

Missed messages

Clients ask for appointments while you are busy working.

Messy scheduling

Appointments get mixed between chats, notes, and calendars.

No-shows

Clients forget their appointment when there is no clear reminder.

Too much manual work

You spend time confirming, organizing, and answering the same questions.

How the app helps

The booking app gives your business a clean public booking page where clients can choose a service, pick an available time, and send a request without back-and-forth messages.

  • Public booking page
  • Services, prices, and availability
  • Simple appointment management
  • Client reminders
  • Cleaner workflow for daily bookings

Built for barbershops, salons, nail artists, spas, beauty professionals, and other businesses that work by appointment.

PUBLIC BOOKING PAGE

Clients can choose a service, pick a time, and request an appointment from a clean public page.

BOOKING LINK & QR

Share your booking link or QR code so clients can book without back-and-forth messages.
